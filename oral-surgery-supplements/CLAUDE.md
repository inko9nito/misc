# Oral surgery supplement tracker

Read HANDOFF.md first. It is the source of truth for doses, dates, design rules and open questions.

- Don't change a dose or schedule without checking "Open questions" in HANDOFF.md.
- Keep the design rules in "Design direction" (minimal UI, sentence case, reference-sized).
- No prices and no appointment time anywhere.
- To update the live tracker, republish app/surgery-checklist.html to the same artifact URL.
