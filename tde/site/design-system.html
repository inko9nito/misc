Rules

Do

  • Keep the wordmark, purple carbon and red; modernize how they’re used.
  • Put everything below the hero on white.
  • Use the racing font for headings only, in sentence case.
  • Use real track images and real car photos.
  • Match the card thumbnail to the event-page banner.
  • Pair every group color with its name.

Don’t

  • Show the 1-day and 2-day color sets on the same event.
  • Number the run groups or call them levels.
  • Use orange for UI or selection states.
  • Draw made-up track shapes.
  • Use all caps for headings, or the racing font on card details.
  • Squeeze text into narrow columns, or add tables, gauges or facts nobody asked for.
