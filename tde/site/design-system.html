TDE design system · v1

Precision, in TDE purple

Built on the BMW M foundation from Open Design Agent Kit: square geometry, light large display type, spaced uppercase labels, spec cells and photography on dark bands. Carried in TDE’s own purple, red and racing wordmark, with no BMW marks. Every sample here is rendered by the site’s own CSS and JS.

05 · Space and shape

Square, ruled, generous

No rounded corners. Structure comes from 1px rules: an ink rule opens a block, hairlines separate rows. Bands are 112px apart on desktop, 64px on phones. Touch targets are at least 44px.

10 · Rules

Do and don’t

Do

  • Keep TDE’s wordmark, purple and red.
  • Put everything people read on white.
  • Lead with real car photos and real track outlines.
  • Set headings light and large, with one strong phrase at most.
  • Use square corners and 1px rules for structure.
  • Pair every group color with its name.

Don’t

  • Copy BMW: no tricolor stripe, no BMW type, no all-caps headlines.
  • Show the 1-day and 2-day color sets on the same event.
  • Number the run groups or call them levels.
  • Use orange for UI or selection.
  • Draw made-up track shapes.
  • Add rounded corners, drop shadows or gradients on UI.
