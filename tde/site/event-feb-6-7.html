About this event

TDE’s full weekend school: two days on a true racetrack, with more seat time and more classroom than the 1-day format. Open to all levels, Novice through Advanced, with certified in-car coaching and classroom sessions on precision, speed and control.

Format
Two full days on a true racetrack
Track time
4 driving sessions per run group each day (8 in total), plus 2 lead/follow sessions
Coaching
Certified in-car instructors coach and evaluate you
Classroom
Sessions for every run group between track sessions
Levels
Novice through Advanced, in four run groups

Run groups

Typical groupings for this 2-day format. Placement depends on your experience, history references, credible driver evaluations and the overall mix of drivers, and TDE makes the final call.

  • GreenNew drivers. Full-time in-car instruction.At 1-day events: Purple
  • BlueBuilding consistency. Full-time instruction; some drivers earn part-time solo.At 1-day events: Purple if you’re new to Blue, Orange if part-time instructed
  • YellowExperienced. Part-time instruction, or solo once TDE approves you.At 1-day events: Orange, or Pink if TDE solo-approved
  • RedMost advanced. Solo to TDE standards.At 1-day events: Pink
Am I solo-approved? (and the solo discount)

Drivers TDE has approved to solo in Yellow, Red or Pink get a reduced event fee. You’re not solo-approved if any of these apply:

  • TDE hasn’t evaluated and approved you yet. You’re eligible for Green, Blue or non-solo Yellow.
  • You’re new to TDE.
  • You haven’t driven this event, track, configuration or direction.
  • You haven’t driven with TDE in over 12 months. You’ll be re-evaluated.
  • TDE decides you need, or you want, more in-car instruction.

TDE’s run-group and solo standards are among the highest in the industry; other HPDE organizations qualify drivers differently.
