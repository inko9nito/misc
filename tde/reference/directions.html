    About this event

    The same TDE benchmark school in a high-intensity, one-day format, built for drivers who want maximum seat time. Open to all levels, Novice through Advanced, with elite in-car coaching and focused classroom sessions on precision, speed and control.

    Format
    One full day on a true racetrack
    Track time
    5 driving sessions per run group, plus lead/follow lapping at lunch
    Coaching
    Certified in-car instructors coach and evaluate you
    Classroom
    Sessions for every run group between track sessions
    Levels
    Novice through Advanced, in three run groups

    Run groups

    Typical groupings for this 1-day format. Placement depends on your experience, history references, credible driver evaluations and the overall mix of drivers — and TDE makes the final call.

    • PurpleNew drivers. Full-time in-car instruction.At 2-day schools: Green, and Blue drivers new to that group
    • OrangeProven, credible experience. Part-time instruction.At 2-day schools: Yellow and Blue, part-time instructed
    • PinkAdvanced. Full-time solo to TDE standards.At 2-day schools: TDE solo-approved Yellow and Red
    Am I solo-approved? (and the solo discount)

    Drivers TDE has approved to solo in Yellow, Red or Pink get a reduced event fee. You’re not solo-approved if any of these apply:

    • TDE hasn’t evaluated and approved you yet — you’re eligible for Green, Blue or non-solo Yellow
    • You’re new to TDE
    • You haven’t driven this event, track, configuration or direction
    • You haven’t driven with TDE in over 12 months — you’ll be re-evaluated
    • TDE decides you need, or you want, more in-car instruction

    TDE’s run-group and solo standards are among the highest in the industry; other HPDE organizations qualify drivers differently.

    New to TDE but have track or race experience?

    Fill in the details on your TDE driver profile. TDE verifies them and evaluates you in the car at the event to confirm your group or solo eligibility. More in the FAQ.
